# Master Resume

Everything you have ever done goes here. Length does not matter and nobody else reads this file.
Each tailored resume picks from it, so an item left out here can never appear on a resume.
Write facts and numbers. Rough wording is fine.

## Contact

- Full name
- City and region
- Phone
- Email
- LinkedIn
- Website, portfolio, or GitHub

## Education

One entry per school, newest first.

### School, City
- Degree and major, minor or specialization
- Start date and graduation date (or expected date)
- GPA and scale, class rank, honors
- Test scores (SAT, ACT, GMAT, GRE) if you would ever list them
- Every relevant course
- Scholarships, exchange terms, thesis title

## Work Experience

One entry per job, internship, contract, or freelance client, newest first. Include part-time and
unrelated jobs.

### Job Title, Employer, City
- Start and end dates (month and year)
- Paid or unpaid, full-time or part-time, team size, who you reported to
- Everything you did, one bullet each. For every bullet give the tools used and a number where one
  exists (money, percent, time saved, volume, people, rank).
- Deals, transactions, clients, or accounts you worked on, with size and your part in each
- Promotions and awards in the role

## Projects

Class projects, personal projects, research, open source, case competitions, hackathons.

### Project Name, Your Role
- Dates
- What it is, what you built or analyzed, with which tools
- Result (users, grade, placement, link)

## Leadership and Activities

Clubs, student government, teams, athletics, volunteering, community roles.

### Organization, Your Role
- Dates
- What you ran, how many people, how much money, what changed

## Skills

List everything, grouped any way you like. Give a level where levels differ.

- Technical tools and software
- Programming languages and frameworks
- Finance, accounting, and analysis methods
- Lab, design, or trade skills
- Spoken languages with proficiency

## Certifications and Licenses

- Name, issuer, date, status (passed, candidate, in progress), expiry

## Awards and Honors

- Award, issuer, date, what it was for, how selective

## Publications and Presentations

- Title, venue, date, co-authors, link

## Interests

Specific ones. "Marathon running, 3 completed" beats "sports".
